Questions, answered

The questions travel businesses ask us most before they start a project.

What is the difference between white-label and custom?

White-label is our ready-made platform, configured with your branding, modules and suppliers, so it launches faster and costs less. Custom is built around your own workflows when off-the-shelf software won't fit.

How long does a launch take?

That depends on the modules and supplier integrations you choose. White-label projects usually take weeks. Custom projects are planned in milestones over months. You get a timeline with your proposal.

Do I need my own supplier contracts?

Usually, yes. GDS, airline, hotel and other suppliers contract directly with your business. We handle the technical integration, certification and testing once you have access.

Can I sell my own fares and hotels?

Yes. The extranet lets you load series fares, private fares, contracted hotels and packages, and sell them alongside supplier inventory.

Who owns the bookings and payments?

You do. Elyra is the software provider. Bookings, payments, refunds and customer support stay with your business, through your own payment gateway.

Can you build mobile apps too?

Yes. We build Android and iOS booking apps for travellers and agents. They connect to the same platform as your website.

What happens after launch?

We offer maintenance and support plans, plus optional hosting and cloud management, so your platform stays secure and up to date.
